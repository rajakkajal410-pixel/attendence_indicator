Overview

Traditional attendance systems are often manual, time-consuming and limited to recording student presence. They provide little information about classroom interaction or session effectiveness. The Multimodal Attendance & Engagement Agent addresses these challenges through an AI-powered classroom intelligence system. It uses computer vision, audio signal processing and agent-based reasoning to generate attendance records, analyze engagement trends and provide useful teaching suggestions. Designed for offline operation and privacy-conscious deployment, the system helps educators understand classroom dynamics and make informed decisions that support a more effective learning experience.

🔒 Privacy-Conscious AI

Designed to operate offline, with responsible handling of classroom data and human oversight of teaching decisions.

Project Scenarios

📋

01. Automated Attendance

Processes live or recorded classroom video to identify participants and track sustained presence. It generates timestamped attendance logs and reduces the need for manual roll calls.

📊

02. Engagement Analysis

Analyzes permitted behavioral indicators, movement patterns and audio activity to identify engagement trends, participation peaks and possible attention drops during a session.

🤖

03. AI Teaching Suggestions

Provides context-aware suggestions to help instructors adjust lesson pacing, introduce interactive activities and improve classroom management.
